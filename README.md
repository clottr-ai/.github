# Clottr

Clottr is an AI-powered virtual try-on and daily outfit planner app. It reads your wardrobe, the weather, and your calendar, and suggests a complete outfit every morning from clothes you already own. Try on new pieces before you buy, check whether a purchase actually fills a gap in your closet, and pack for trips with a day-by-day plan.

**Website:** [clottr.ai](https://clottr.ai/) · **Platforms:** iOS and Android coming soon. Browser extension after.

## Features

### Virtual Try-On
Virtual Try-On shows you wearing any item or full outfit from a single selfie. Upload one photo and Clottr's AI fits the piece to your pose, matches your face, and renders a realistic preview in seconds. You never need a new photo for each try-on. It works with clothes from your own wardrobe or from any online store.

### AI Outfit Planner
The AI Outfit Planner builds a complete outfit every morning from clothes you already own. It checks your local weather forecast, reads your calendar, and matches the formality to your day before recommending a look. Every outfit is scored across seven style dimensions: color harmony, formality match, proportion balance, style coherence, pattern mixing, occasion, and seasonal fit, and only looks that pass all of them are shown.

### Should I Buy
Should I Buy tells you whether a new purchase is worth it before you spend. Paste a link or snap a photo and Clottr checks the item against your whole wardrobe for duplicates, calculates how many outfits you could make with it, projects cost per wear, and weighs it against your style rules and the season. You get a plain verdict, for example, that you already own three similar navy knits, or that a piece fills a real gap in your closet.

### Digital Wardrobe
The Digital Wardrobe catalogs your closet automatically. Snap a single item, photograph a full outfit or a rack to extract every garment at once, or import product images from any store, the AI removes the background and sorts every piece into roughly 19 attributes, from category, color, and material to fit, formality, and season. It also tracks cost per wear per item as your wears are logged.

### Trip Planner
The Trip Planner packs for you around the real forecast. Tell Clottr where you're going, when, and what you have planned, and it pulls the multi-day forecast (up to six months out), builds an outfit for each day from your own wardrobe, and turns them into a single packing list with nothing double-packed. If the weather or your plans change mid-trip, it re-suggests the affected day and updates the list.

### Wardrobe Insights
Wardrobe Insights reads your closet back to you by the numbers. It surfaces your color palette, most and least-worn pieces, cost per wear, streaks, and peer percentiles, written as a stylist rather than a dashboard. It flags deep cuts you haven't worn in months when the weather finally suits them.

### AI Stylist Chat
AI Stylist Chat answers what to wear and then builds the outfit for you. Ask about a specific occasion. A city-hall wedding, an 11am client meeting and it matches the formality, checks the forecast, assembles a look from clothes you actually own, and renders it on you. It only ever uses items from your catalogued wardrobe, and every look is validated by a rule engine, so it never suggests pieces you don't have.

### Browser Extension
The Clottr browser extension brings try-on and buy-checks to every online store (coming soon). Hover over any product image and you can try the item on your photo, run a Should I Buy check against your wardrobe, add it to your digital wardrobe, or drop it onto the outfit canvas — all without leaving the page. Clottr never earns commission on purchases, so the advice is about whether you need an item, not where to buy it cheaper.

### More features
- **Shop My Gaps** - Pinpoints the pieces your wardrobe is missing so each new purchase closes a real gap.
- **Rate My Outfit** - Grades any look across seven style dimensions and hands back candid strengths and fixes.
- **Muse** - Reworks an inspiration photo into an outfit you can actually wear, drawn from clothes you own.
- **Studio** - Builds shareable outfit graphics and restyles your looks with AI art filters.
- **Style DNA** - A short onboarding quiz, reference photos, and a captured pose set your formality range, color leanings, fabric tolerances, and fit preferences; every recommendation builds on it.

## FAQ

### What is Clottr?
Clottr is an AI-powered virtual try-on and daily outfit planner app. Day to day it turns the clothes you already own into a morning outfit chosen for the weather and your schedule, previews new pieces on your body before you buy, and tells you whether a purchase earns a place in your closet.

### Is Clottr free?
Yes, Clottr has a free tier. Each month it includes 25 credits to use for try-ons, Should I Buy checks, or outfit generation, while cataloguing your wardrobe and managing fits stays unlimited at no cost.

### How does the virtual try-on work?
You give Clottr one selfie, and its AI renders a lifelike preview of you in any single garment or complete outfit, usually within a few seconds. That first photo is reused for everything afterward, so there's no re-uploading per item, and it works whether the piece comes from your own closet or a store page online.

### How does the outfit planner work?
Each day the planner weighs four things together: what's in your wardrobe, the local forecast, what's on your calendar, and the style rules you've set and assembles full outfits from them. Before any look reaches you it's graded on seven fronts (color harmony, formality match, proportion balance, style coherence, pattern mixing, occasion, and seasonal fit), and anything that fails a check is dropped.

### What platforms is Clottr on?
Clottr is a mobile app on both iOS and Android. A companion browser extension for shopping on any online store is landing shortly after launch, and it will be included for every user.

### Which stores does it support?
Any of them. Clottr runs on every online store from Zara, H&M, and ASOS to Nordstrom, Amazon, and thousands of others, because it works from the product image itself: if you can see it on the page, Clottr can read it and put it on you. For physcial shops, photograph the item and run the same checks in the app.

### Is my data private?
Yes. Your photo and your catalogued clothes are held securely and only ever feed your own recommendations and try-ons, and you can export or delete everything whenever you want. The browser extension is just as contained: it activates only on the sites you've approved and stays dormant everywhere else you browse. Clottr is based in the EU, built by enterprise architects, so data protection is part of our core system.
